Aevis
Task-based review.

Observe people who actually do the relevant work. Begin with their current tools and recent behavior, then evaluate the proposed task. This page prepares a study; it contains no participant findings.

Three tasks to investigate

  1. Set the audit context

    Prepare an audit for a sample brand and explain which context will be used.

    Observe: Preset consistency, custom edits and query relevance.
  2. Interpret the result

    Explain what the result establishes, where it came from and what remains uncertain.

    Observe: Simulation comprehension, denominator/source interpretation and overconfidence.
  3. Evaluate a suggestion

    Review proposed content and explain what you would check before publishing it.

    Observe: Evidence use, approval boundary and understanding of a simulated retest.

Capture behavior before interpretation.

Record the design version, task, what happened, any exact quote you have permission to retain, your interpretation, severity, proposed change and the focused retest.

Report qualitative counts with participant context. A small study does not establish a population conversion rate or a business impact claim.

Execution checklist

  • Recruit relevant people outside the design team. Include varied devices, confidence and access needs.
  • Explain the task and the sample nature of the prototype. Obtain any necessary recording permission.
  • Use neutral prompts and sample data. Avoid teaching the intended route.
  • Keep the original design, actual observation, revised version and retest together.
  • Publish only permissioned, anonymized evidence. Keep uncertain interpretations separate from observations.
Comparative tasks

Use equivalent tasks in the participant’s existing method and this design. Record order, conditions, assistance and correction effort before describing a difference.
