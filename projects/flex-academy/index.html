01 / The challenge

One page.
Two different decisions.

I was tasked with creating a Flex Academy landing page that would perform. Working across UX, UI, and marketing meant connecting the offer, the evidence, and the action a visitor should take.

The brief describes beginners looking for a starting sequence and operators looking to improve an existing business. The page needed to explain a new education offer while making both paths easy to find.

Design question

How can we earn trust and help each visitor choose a next step that matches their stage?

Launch / Starting out

“Where do I begin?”

A clear introduction to the model, first units, and operating foundations.

Free webinar

Intended next step: learn before considering the programme.

Scale / Already operating

“How do I make this repeatable?”

Systems, automation, and delegation for an existing rental operation.

Strategy call

Intended next step: discuss fit before committing.

Audience needs are working hypotheses from the client brief. They have not yet been validated through participant interviews.

03 / Design decisions

Every section has a job.

The saved design moves from understanding the offer to trusting its source, recognising the right path, and evaluating the commitment.

01

Lead with Launch. Keep Scale within reach.

A prominent webinar action follows the brief's beginner priority. A contextual operator link near the hero and an explicit path selector keep the second audience visible.

TradeoffA dominant beginner message could make operators assume the offer is irrelevant.

Test nextCan each audience choose an appropriate route and explain what happens next?

02

Explain the proof, as well as displaying it.

The Flex Base360 Academy sequence makes unfamiliar brands meaningful. Ecosystem testimonials need clear attribution so operational credibility is not mistaken for learner success.

TradeoffCompact metrics establish scale, but may not explain why a programme is useful.

Test nextCan visitors explain the founders' relevance and distinguish operating proof from learning outcomes?

03

Show the method behind the promise.

Launch presents a learning sequence. Scale describes systemisation, automation, and delegation. The content gives visitors a mechanism to evaluate alongside programme support.

TradeoffA detailed page can answer objections, but repeated sections may delay action.

Test nextDoes a shorter version preserve comprehension, relevance, and qualified interest?

05 / Validation & iteration

A clear plan to challenge the design.

The retrospective review gives us specific issues to investigate. Human testing is prepared for execution, with actual participant feedback still pending.

Completed / Expert review

What the materials establish

  • Distinct webinar and strategy-call routes.
  • Attributed ecosystem proof.
  • A contrast issue in a documented color pair.
  • Local forms explicitly marked as demos.
  • Unconfirmed prices and webinar timing.

Prepared / Participant research

What needs real feedback

  • Five beginners and five operators in an initial qualitative round.
  • Offer comprehension and route-selection tasks.
  • Trust evaluation and conversion-journey tasks.
  • Observed behavior, quotes, severity, and prioritized revisions.
  • A focused retest after changes.

Priorities for the next iteration

  1. Clarify the boundary. A one-unit operator may fit either audience; pair stage with the visitor's goal.
  2. Confirm the commitment. Publish approved offer details and timing, or offer truthful notifications while scheduling is unresolved.
  3. Complete the journey. Verify registration, qualification, availability, confirmation, and recovery states.

06 / Business measurement

Measure the commitment beyond the click.

More leads are valuable when they translate into relevant attendance and enrollment. The two paths need separate measurement, with device and traffic-source context.

Launch funnel

From introduction to enrollment

  1. Eligible visit
  2. Completed webinar registration
  3. Webinar attendance
  4. Programme enrollment

Primary page measure: successful unique registrations per eligible visitor.

Scale funnel

From fit to a qualified conversation

  1. Eligible visit
  2. Qualification and booking
  3. Qualified attended call
  4. Programme enrollment

Primary page measure: qualified bookings per eligible visitor.

Guardrails: lead quality, attendance, acquisition cost, cancellations, and delivery capacity. Live A/B experiments require sufficient traffic, stable randomisation, and a predefined primary outcome.

DECISIONS AND TRADEOFFS

Why this behavior earns its place.

A beginner priority, with an operator route

Giving every offer equal prominence would blur the first decision. The saved landing page gives the beginner webinar priority. The later portfolio study adds a responsive offer summary, attributed operating context and two explicit goals to let a reviewer inspect either complete journey.

Tradeoff. A one-unit operator can belong to either route. The interaction asks for the person’s goal rather than inferring it from unit count.

Confirm the commitment

A working-looking form can imply a real date or booking. The local study labels sample availability, preserves input and shows confirmation and recovery.

Tradeoff. The study demonstrates interaction behavior. Real scheduling and approved offer details remain client dependencies.

Distinguish kinds of proof

Operating scale and software capability explain the founders’ context. They do not establish student results.

Tradeoff. Attribution costs space, but protects comprehension and supports an informed decision.

07 / Current outcome & reflection

Make the next commitment explicit.

The reviewed design connects operator credibility to two relevant commitments. Its commercial impact still needs live measurement.

Keep. Keep beginner learning and operator advice as separate routes, chosen by the visitor’s goal. The local study makes both commitments and their recovery states inspectable.

Change next. Carry the current study’s readable action colors, retained form details and explicit confirmation into the client-approved design version. Confirm the webinar schedule, offer terms and actual booking service first.

Question to test. Can beginners and operators explain which route fits, what happens after the action and what the ecosystem proof actually establishes? Audience testing and client feedback are still needed.
