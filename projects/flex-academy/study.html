What counts as a confirmed outcome?

registration_confirmed
Authority: Approved webinar/registration service
Confirmed service request ID and accepted status. A button click, form review, retry or existing request is not a new registration.
Not connected in this study. Approved webinar schedule and delivery-service details are not supplied here.
qualified_booking_confirmed
Authority: Booking service plus a separate qualification record
An accepted booking and an explicit qualification decision under client-approved criteria. A time selection or Scale route alone is insufficient.
Not connected. Qualification criteria, review ownership and the production booking service require client approval.
webinar_attended / call_attended
Authority: Attendance record from the approved event/call service
Join attendance to the accepted request ID. Report the cohort, period, denominator and missing attendance records.
No attendance integration or real attendance results.

Use distinct authoritative service request IDs, a defined acquisition cohort and observation window. Retain cancellations and qualification changes; do not sum repeated confirmation events.
