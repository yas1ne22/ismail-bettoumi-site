Behind the interaction

Self-initiated motion concept. Four shapes retain their identity as they move between three spatial arrangements. Staggered timing and a spring easing curve give each transition weight. Adjust the tempo or replay a transition. Reduced-motion preferences replace movement with immediate state changes.
