THE IDEA

A design language.
Across languages.

What does an AI interface look like when Arabic is its starting point? Nasaq combines editorial character with the calm structure of a useful interface, then extends that language into English.

From ink and paper to conversations and assistants. One system leaves room for the idea and gives each detail its place.
