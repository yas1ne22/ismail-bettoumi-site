Product thesis

Voice input should feel like a layer, not another destination.

Most dictation tools add modes, history, settings, and context capture around a small writing job. Voicy stays invisible until needed, then expands only as the decision becomes richer.

01

Speak before choosing

Intent is inferred after transcription, so the person does not have to predict the correct mode before they know what they want.

02

Preview before effect

Every transformation becomes editable text. Nothing is inserted, sent, or executed without explicit approval.

03

No hidden context

Version one does not read the screen, clipboard, selected text, files, or conversation history. The transcript is the input.

Core experience

From invisible to useful in four states.

Motion preserves spatial continuity. The surface expands upward from a bottom-center capsule and never becomes a full application window.

STATE 01

Rest without visual noise

A 52 by 8 point bottom-center hotspot, the menu bar, and the keyboard shortcut keep Voicy reachable. At rest, the desktop shows no visible pixels.

STATE 02

Hold to capture

Option + Space opens a focused listening capsule with waveform, elapsed state, Stop, and Escape. The passive surface does not steal keyboard focus.

Trust boundary

Clear about what moves, where, and why.

The experience names each provider state and limits the system to text transformations. Useful automation begins with disciplined constraints.

On-device firstAudio capture and target resolution begin locally. Capture ends completely before any provider upload.
Keychain-held credentialThe owner’s API key is non-synchronizing secret material and never enters logs, evidence, or accessibility text.
Text actions onlyEmail means a subject and body, not send. AI Prompt means a structured request, not execution.
One safe commitTarget revalidation happens immediately before insertion. Ambiguity stops the flow.

Product reflection

Magic is better when its boundaries are visible.

Voicy’s differentiator is not speech recognition alone. It is the choreography of trust: reveal at the right moment, explain the state, preserve control, then disappear.
