This form creates a downloadable note. It sends nothing to a server and does not save notes automatically. Use anonymous participant IDs and include only material you have permission to retain.

Evidence stays separate.

  • Describe actions and assistance before explaining a motivation.
  • Keep negative observations in the record.
  • Name whether a session used a static reference, interactive study or live product.
  • Record unexecuted retests as pending.
  • A note export does not publish a study or establish completion.
